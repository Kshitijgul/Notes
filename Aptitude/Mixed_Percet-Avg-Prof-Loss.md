# 📘 CONCEPT INTEGRATION MAP

## How These Three Topics Connect

```
PERCENTAGE
    ↕           ↕
AVERAGE  ←→  PROFIT & LOSS
```

| Connection | How They Mix |
|-----------|-------------|
| % + Average | Average as a %, % change in average, weighted average |
| % + P&L | Profit% on CP, discount%, markup% |
| Average + P&L | Average CP, average SP, average profit% |
| All Three | Average profit% using weighted average with % change |

## Quick Formula Recall

$$\text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum of observations}}{\text{Number of observations}}$$

$$\text{Profit\%} = \frac{SP - CP}{CP} \times 100$$

$$\text{\% Change in Average} = \frac{\text{New Avg} - \text{Old Avg}}{\text{Old Avg}} \times 100$$

$$\text{Weighted Average} = \frac{\sum w_i x_i}{\sum w_i}$$

## Question 1

**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Expected Exam:** SSC CGL / RRB / IBPS Clerk
**Concepts Used:** Percentage + Average
**Topic(s) Used:** Percentage, Average
**Hidden Concept:** Average of percentage scores ≠ percentage of total (unless equal max marks)

---

### Question Statement

A student scored 60%, 70%, and 80% in three subjects. Each subject has maximum marks of 100. Find his average score and overall percentage.

---

### Given

- Scores: 60, 70, 80 (out of 100 each)
- Total maximum = 300

### Required

Average score and overall percentage

---

### Concept Identification

**Percentage + Average** — When maximum marks are equal, average% = average of individual %s.

**5-second Pattern:** Equal max marks → average% = (sum of %s)/n

---

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1:** Total marks scored = 60 + 70 + 80 = 210

**Step 2:** Average score = 210/3 = **70**

**Step 3:** Overall percentage = (210/300) × 100 = **70%**

**Step 4:** Average of %s = (60 + 70 + 80)/3 = 210/3 = **70%** (same — because max marks are equal)

---

### Key Insight

When maximum marks are EQUAL across subjects, average of individual percentages = overall percentage. When UNEQUAL, they differ.

---

### Common Mistakes

- Assuming average% always equals average of individual %s — only true for equal max marks ❌

---

### Final Answer

**Average Score = 70 | Overall Percentage = 70%**

### Question Statement

A shopkeeper bought 3 articles for ₹200, ₹300, and ₹400. He sold all three at a uniform price of ₹350 each. Find his average cost price and overall profit or loss%.

---

### Given

- CPs: ₹200, ₹300, ₹400
- SP of each = ₹350

---

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1:** Total CP = 200 + 300 + 400 = ₹900

**Step 2:** Average CP = 900/3 = **₹300**

**Step 3:** Total SP = 3 × 350 = ₹1,050

**Step 4:** Total Profit = 1,050 − 900 = ₹150

**Step 5:** Profit% = (150/900) × 100 = **16.67%**

**Alternative using average:**

Average CP = ₹300, SP = ₹350

Profit% = (350 − 300)/300 × 100 = 50/300 × 100 = **16.67%** ✓

---

### Final Answer

**Average CP = ₹300 | Overall Profit% = 16.67%**

## Question 26

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Expected Exam:** IBPS PO / SBI PO
**Concepts Used:** Average + Profit & Loss + Percentage + Unequal CP
**Topic(s) Used:** All three
**Hidden Concept:** Overall profit% ≠ average of individual profit%s when CPs differ

---

### Question Statement

A trader buys 3 items. Item A: CP = ₹200, sold at 30% profit. Item B: CP = ₹500, sold at 20% profit. Item C: CP = ₹300, sold at 10% loss. Find his overall profit% and compare with simple average of the three profit%s.

---

### Step-by-Step Solution

**Individual SPs:**

SP_A = 200 × 1.30 = ₹260

SP_B = 500 × 1.20 = ₹600

SP_C = 300 × 0.90 = ₹270

**Total CP = 1,000, Total SP = 1,130**

**Overall Profit% = (130/1000) × 100 = 13%**

**Simple average of profit%s = (30 + 20 − 10)/3 = 40/3 = 13.33%**

**They are different** (13% ≠ 13.33%)

---

### Weighted Average Method

$$\text{Overall\%} = \frac{200 \times 30 + 500 \times 20 + 300 \times (-10)}{200 + 500 + 300}$$

$$= \frac{6000 + 10000 - 3000}{1000} = \frac{13000}{1000} = 13\%$$

---

### Final Answer

**Overall Profit% = 13% | Simple Average = 13.33% | They differ when CPs are unequal**
