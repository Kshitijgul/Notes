# ⭐ Quality Requirements
### Learning-First Rule (Mandatory)

Do NOT create separate large sections for theory and solved questions.

Instead, after every concept, formula, rule, shortcut, or theorem:

1. Explain the concept in the simplest possible way (maximum 5–10 lines).
2. Immediately give 2–5 progressively difficult examples:
   - Easy
   - Medium
   - Hard
   - Previous Year–style (if applicable)
3. Show the complete step-by-step solution.
4. Explain why the chosen method works.
5. Highlight the common mistake students make.
6. Show the fastest shortcut (if one exists).
7. Provide 2–3 unsolved practice questions before moving to the next concept.

The notes should follow this learning sequence:

Concept → Intuition → Formula → Solved Examples → Trick → Common Mistake → Mini Practice → Next Concept

Avoid long uninterrupted theory sections. The emphasis should be on learning through carefully selected examples, with theory serving only to support problem solving.


The generated notes must:

- Be self-contained.
- Cover theory + practice.
- Explain concepts from first principles.
- Prioritize exam-oriented methods over lengthy proofs.
- Emphasize the fastest solving techniques.
- Avoid repetitive content.
- Progress logically from Beginner → Advanced.
- Be suitable for both learning and last-minute revision.
- Be polished enough to resemble premium coaching institute material.

The student should not need any additional source after studying these notes.
